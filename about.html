A little about me

Hi, I'm Jana

I'm an ESL coach from Lebanon, and I work one-to-one with Arabic speakers — online, anywhere in the world.

I've been teaching English for over five years in secondary schools, universities, and NGOs across Lebanon and Turkey. I founded English Mastered in 2021 because I kept meeting the same student: an Arabic speaker who had studied English for years and still froze the moment they had to use it.

I know that freeze. Arabic is my first language, and I learned English exactly the way my students do. I know the sound your mouth defaults to, the word order your brain reaches for, and the rule that should work but doesn't — and that's the whole reason English Mastered exists.

Whether you're preparing for IELTS, writing emails at work, or you just want to hold a conversation without panicking — I'll meet you exactly where you are and build a clear path from there.
