Common questions

Everything you might want to know before your free call.

Who is English Mastered for?

English Mastered is built for Arabic-speaking students and professionals who want to improve their English. Jana is a native Arabic speaker and ESL coach from Lebanon, so she understands the specific grammar, pronunciation, and fluency challenges Arabic speakers face — because she's been through them herself.

What can I learn with Jana?

One-to-one coaching in General English, IELTS and TOEFL preparation, Business English, English for Specific Purposes (maritime, medical, legal, aviation), and Conversation & Fluency. Every level is welcome, from complete beginner (A1) through to advanced proficiency (C2).

How much do lessons cost?

A single 60-minute lesson is $15. The 5-lesson package is $65–70 and the 10-lesson package is $115–125, depending on how you pay — paying direct (Whish, Western Union/OMT, or USDT) gets you the lower price. No contracts, no hidden fees. See all packages →

How do I prepare for IELTS as an Arabic speaker?

IELTS prep for Arabic speakers means focusing on where Arabic and English diverge most — Writing Task 2 essay structure, Speaking fluency and pronunciation, and Reading speed. Jana builds your lessons around the exact points where Arabic speakers most often lose band score, rather than a generic IELTS syllabus.

Are lessons online or in person?

Every lesson is online, via Zoom, Teams, or Webex — so you can join from Lebanon, the Gulf, Europe, or anywhere else, on a timezone that suits you.
